# DX Pilot release history

## 0.5.0 — 21 October 2026

- Matching firmware for the 2.8-inch CYD and 3.5-inch ESP32-3248S035C.
- HF propagation reports and maps, DX spots, shack clock and weather.
- Personal ADIF/LoTW DXCC tracking, independent rarity alerts and custom clusters.
- On-device and local web settings, Wi-Fi updates and optional device counting.
- Both builds installed and checked on real hardware, with saved settings preserved.

Website and installer: [www.dxpilot.co.uk](https://www.dxpilot.co.uk/).
